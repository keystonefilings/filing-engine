# Florida (Sunbiz), walked live 2026-11-19

Florida's Division of Corporations online filing (efile.sunbiz.org) needs no login to
file, and it was walked end to end with test data, stopping before payment. The engine's
Florida adapter was then dry-run against the live site and completed all five screens,
stopping at the Payment Page (tracking number issued, nothing paid, nothing filed).
Only the entity search (search.sunbiz.org) is behind a Cloudflare challenge; the filing
flow is not.

## Domestic LLC: Articles of Organization ($125)

Entry: efile.sunbiz.org/llc_file.html. Flow:
1. Disclaimer page: accept the checkbox, "Start New Filing".
2. One long form (coretype.exe): effective date (optional), Certificate of Status (+$5)
   and Certified Copy (+$30) checkboxes, LLC name, principal address, mailing address
   ("same as principal" checkbox), registered agent (individual name or a business name,
   a Florida street address, and the agent's signature), purpose (optional, 240 chars),
   correspondence name and email (entered twice), electronic signature, and up to six
   optional authorized persons (off1-off6) each with a title code (MGR, AMBR, AP, AR),
   name or entity name, and address.
3. Review page (coredisp.exe): all values shown for verification.
4. Pre-payment summary (corefile.exe): a document tracking number and the $125 charge.
5. Payment Page (corenrtn.exe): **Credit Card Payment**, or a prepaid **Sunbiz E-file
   account** (account number + password + email). The Sunbiz E-file account is Florida's
   equivalent of New York's drawdown account.

Fee: $125 required ($100 filing + $25 registered agent designation), + $5 Certificate of
Status, + $30 Certified Copy. Name must contain an LLC indicator. The registered agent
must have a Florida address. Authorized persons are optional.

## Profit Corporation — Articles of Incorporation ($70), walked to payment

Entry: efile.sunbiz.org/profit_file.html. Same flow as the LLC. Adds **corporate stock
shares** (required), an **incorporator** (name + address + electronic signature), a
"Corporate purpose is 'Any and all lawful business'" checkbox or a purpose statement, and
up to six optional officers/directors (off1-off6) with a title. Fee $70; Certificate of
Status +$8.75, Certified Copy +$52.50. The fl-profit-corporation adapter dry-run passed
all five screens and stopped before payment.

## Limited Partnership — Certificate ($1000), walked to payment

Entry: efile.sunbiz.org/lp_file.html. Same flow. Name must end in LP/LLLP; an optional
LLLP election checkbox; at least one **general partner**, each with a name or entity name,
an entity document number (for an entity GP), address, and a **signature**. Fee $1000;
Status +$8.75, Certified Copy +$52.50. The fl-limited-partnership adapter dry-run passed
all five screens and stopped before payment.

## Engine status

- `fl-formation`, `fl-profit-corporation` and `fl-limited-partnership` adapters, one per
  Florida entity type (floridaEntityType in the request routes to the right one). All
  three dry-run passed live against Sunbiz (5 screens each, fee check ok, stopped before
  "Credit Card Payment"). complete:false until a verified real filing. Fields are filled
  by exact HTML name because Florida's flat form repeats labels across sections.
- Fees: FL LLC formation $125 (+$5 status, +$30 certified copy); profit corp $70 and LP
  $1000 recorded under formation.entityFees.
- Settings: `flPaymentMethod` (credit_card or sunbiz_account) and the prepaid
  `flSunbizAccountCredentialKey` (service company's, or a firm's under G1). No login to
  file; card is entered at the human tap.
- Validation: FL name indicator, principal address, Florida registered agent, 240-char
  purpose, at most 6 authorized persons. In-run EIN refused for Florida (examiner review
  is not instant; the EIN runs after state acceptance).
