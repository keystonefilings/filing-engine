# Keystone Filings | New York DOS and IRS EIN filing map

**Review copy · November 18, 2026 · No filings, uploads, document orders, or payments were submitted.**

The New York observations in this map come from Keystone’s signed-in **Division of Corporations Service Company** account. The IRS observations come from the live EIN Assistant through Identity and from published documentation and screen references for later steps.

**Field key** — **R** required, **O** optional, **C** required if the stated branch applies, **D** displayed, derived or prefilled, **V** exact live constraint still to verify. A field can be both **D** and **R**, such as a required address prefilled from the portal account. Proposed Keystone defaults are distinct from current portal settings.

### Important EIN settings and result handling

- **State-first dependency.** IRS directs newly created LLCs and corporations to form at state level before applying for an EIN. For NY, use accepted formation evidence to start the EIN run. Name availability and a pending upload do not satisfy that dependency. An existing and verified EIN can be reused where a later filing asks for it. [IRS EIN eligibility and ordering](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)
- **Keystone’s designee branch.** IRS says a designee receives the EIN through the method used to obtain it, and the IRS mails the official notice to the taxpayer. Record `EIN assigned`, `official notice available digitally`, `official notice mailed`, or `notice capture failed` separately. The same-session download applies when the IRS actually offers the notice. If the current designee session offers a PDF, verify the document type before recording it as the official notice. [IRS Form SS-4 instructions, Third-party designee](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4)
- **One clean IRS pass.** The Assistant reports a 15-minute idle expiration and a limit of one EIN per responsible party per day. Eastern time availability is Mon–Fri 6 a.m.–1 a.m. next day, Sat 6 a.m.–9 p.m., Sun 6 p.m.–midnight. Have the approval operator and complete inputs ready before beginning. An expired pre-submit session can start fresh with the verified input snapshot. A post-submit timeout goes to reconciliation. [IRS EIN Assistant rules and hours](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)
- **Letter recovery.** Keep issued EIN and notice capture as separate facts. If notice capture fails, IRS lists Entity Transcript, eligible Business Tax Account digital CP575, and Letter 147C as verification routes. Preserve the existing assignment. [IRS EIN confirmation options](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/employer-identification-number)

Later-screen sources are the [official Form SS-4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fss4.pdf), [its official instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4), [published EIN interview screen designs](https://brooks-tiffany.com/assets/mod-ein-designs.pdf), [published 2026 LLC interview walkthrough](https://www.llcuniversity.com/irs/apply-for-ein-for-llc-online/), and a [public example of the contemporary IRS assignment screen](https://www.cftc.gov/filings/documents/2026/orgdcmpredcrexhibg10260223.pdf). The latter three corroborate screen behavior, with actual live fields to verify where indicated.
