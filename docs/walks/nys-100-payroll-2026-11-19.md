## Quirks / engine notes
- One form, two agencies (Labor UI + Tax withholding) and three registrations at once.
- The `employerType` radio (Business vs Household) branches the whole form: Business completes
  Parts A,B,D,E; Household completes A,C,D,E-1. The adapter branches on it.
- Part E collects **SSNs** for every owner/partner/member/officer. These are sensitive: mask in
  all screenshots, logs and webhooks (the engine already masks responsible-party SSN — extend
  the same masking to these), and never persist in reusable firm defaults.
- Part E row 1 (owners/officers) and Part E Q2 (locations) are **repeating**; the paper form caps
  at 5 rows / 1 location before "use a separate sheet". The online flow likely uses add-row
  controls — confirm their mechanics on the fixed-IP walk.
- The UI liability trigger is the first quarter with **$300+** cash remuneration (business) or
  **$500+** cash wages (household); do not back-date beyond 3 years from January of the current year.
- FEIN is required and the form says to attach CP 575 — this dovetails with our EIN sub-run
  (file EIN first when einMode=in_run, then register payroll).
- Our existing FilingState/Site enums are NY/NJ/MO/FL(/IRS); a payroll filing type would add a
  new FilingType ("payroll_registration") rather than a new state.
