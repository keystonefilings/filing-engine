# Missouri, mapped from the state's published guides (2026-11-18)

Missouri's online filing system (bsd.sos.mo.gov) sits behind a Cloudflare bot
challenge that refuses data-center IPs, so it could not be walked from the cloud. It
was mapped from Missouri's own step-by-step PDF guides and fee schedule, all reachable
at www.sos.mo.gov. The live walk runs from the fixed-IP machine, like the IRS and NY.

Sources (saved under the walk capture): "Registering a Limited Liability Company",
"Ordering a Certificate of Good Standing", "How to Create an Account", the Schedule of
Fees (fees.pdf), and the LLC amendment form (llc12). All from sos.mo.gov, Nov 18 2026.

## What is online

Missouri runs one account-based system (bsd.sos.mo.gov) for everything: LLC formation,
good standing, annual/biennial reports, fictitious names. A login is required, unlike
NJ. Payment is credit card or electronic check at a shopping cart; there is no drawdown.

## LLC formation ("Create LLC")

Fee: $50 online for a domestic LLC (paper is $105). Screen order from the guide:

1. Sign in; the dashboard shows the LLC options. Choose "Create LLC".
2. Type: confirm "Limited Liability Company" in the dropdown, pick Domestic, "Begin".
3. Details: duration until dissolution, member- or manager-managed, purpose. A "Series
   LLC" checkbox prompts an LLC 1A upload (not mapped; refused for now).
4. Registered agent. Missouri requires a Missouri address, identical to the registered
   office.
5. Organizers: up to 5 online (6 or more needs a paper filing). Organizers need not be
   members or managers. "Save organizer" after each.
6. Principal office address. A PO box is allowed only with a physical street address.
7. Review, with a checkbox to acknowledge the information is true and correct. Each
   section has an "update" button.
8. Signor, with an agreement checkbox confirming the filing is true and correct. This is
   the signature / human-tap moment.
9. "Shopping Cart". Missing required fields raise an error here.
10. Payment: Credit Card or Electronic Check, then fill payment, then the order summary
    and receipt.

Name rule: a Missouri LLC name must contain an LLC indicator (LLC, L.L.C., etc.).

## Good standing

Certificate of Good Standing: $10, flat for all entity types (no NJ-style per-type
pricing). Dashboard "Order Good Standing" in Certificates & Copies, search, select the
entity, "Order Copies/Certificates", add to cart, pay by card or eCheck, then the
certificate PDF is viewable under the order's "View Details".

## Other Missouri fees (from the schedule, for later build order)

- Amendment to an LLC: $25 (penalty fee may apply to a domestic LLC).
- Certificate of Fact: $25. Reservation of Name: $25. Change of Registered Agent: $10.
- Fictitious Name creation / renewal / amendment: $7. Cancellation: no fee.
- Termination / winding up / withdrawal of an LLC: $25 each.

## Engine status

- `mo-formation` adapter and intents built from the guide; `complete: false`, so dry
  runs stop before the cart and real runs are refused until the live walk.
- Fill steps (V14, 2026-11-26): each screen enters what the guide says it asks (type and
  domestic, name, duration, management, purpose, agent, organizers with "Save organizer",
  principal office, the acknowledge and agreement boxes, the cart's payment type). The guide's
  named buttons are used ("Create LLC", "Begin", "Save organizer"); the other screens click a
  single Next or Continue and stop with a message when there is none. Tested only against a
  replica built from the guide (`fixtures/mo-formation`), not against Missouri's pages. The
  sign-in page stops the run: runs use the saved browser session, and typing the stored login
  is not built.
- Fee table: MO formation $50, good standing $10, amendment $25.
- Settings: the service company's `moAccountCredentialKey` (a firm may use its own under
  answer G1); `moPaymentMethod` is credit card or eCheck. Vault key `<tenant>/MO`.
- Validation: MO name indicator, Missouri registered-agent address, management, purpose,
  1 to 5 organizers, principal office, signer; Series LLC refused.
