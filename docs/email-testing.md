# Testing the emails in real inboxes

The owner's choice (Nov 26): no email-testing subscription for now. Resend keeps sending, and the
emails are checked by sending real ones to real inboxes and looking at them. This costs nothing but
an hour.

## Inboxes

Set up one free address at each and read the same emails in each place:

| Inbox | Where to read it | Dark mode |
|---|---|---|
| Gmail | gmail.com in a browser, and the Gmail app on a phone | The app forces dark: turn on the phone's dark theme |
| Outlook | outlook.com in a browser, and Outlook for Windows if anyone has it | outlook.com: Settings, then dark mode; Windows: File, Office Account, Office Theme, Black |
| Apple Mail | Mail on a Mac or an iPhone, with an iCloud address | The system's dark appearance |

## How to send them

Use a test firm on the deployed site (not a real client), with a logo uploaded in Branding. A dark
logo on a transparent background is the one to try: it is what the white plate is for. Then:

1. Put the Gmail address in as the firm's approver, and the Outlook and iCloud addresses as the
   client's contact on a rehearsal.
2. Run the rehearsal through to the end. That sends the proof, the approval, the progress emails
   and documents back, to the firm on CoFile's letterhead and to the client on the firm's.
3. Sign up a test firm and reset its password to get the account emails.

## What to look at in each

- **The logo.** In light mode it sits on the white sheet as in Branding. In the Gmail app with dark
  mode on, the page goes dark and a dark logo stays visible on its small white plate.
- **The button.** It reads clearly and opens the right page.
- **Images off** (Gmail: Settings, "Ask before displaying external images"; Outlook does this by
  default): the firm's name still shows where the logo was, and every value is still there as text.
- **Larger text.** At 200% text size on a phone nothing is cut off.
- **The sender.** Client emails come from the firm's name, and a reply goes to the firm.

Note anything odd with a screenshot and the inbox it came from.
