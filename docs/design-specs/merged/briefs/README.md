# Outsourcing briefs

The work the merged design needs from outside: people, physical things, licenses and testing
([`BUILD-PLAN.md`](../BUILD-PLAN.md) section C). Each brief is written for the vendor, who never sees
this repository: it can be sent as it is once its bracketed items are filled in.

- `[CoFile contact]`: who the vendor deals with.
- `[owner to confirm: …]`: a choice only the owner can make, usually where two parts of the spec differ.
- `[owner to supply: …]`: a number or document only the owner has.

No brief names a price, a budget, a date or a real person.

## Open choices across briefs

Settle each once; the answer goes into every brief that names it.

- **The envelope in the photographs** (04, 08): the letterpress brief sets "Harbor & Pine CPA" as the
  address in Lora 600 with nothing of CoFile; the home page layout puts CoFile's small mark on it as
  the sender, with the address in CoFile's type.
- **Who makes the 2,400 dpi scan of the brass mark plate** (02, 03, 07).
- **Whether Northgate's plate is cut on camera** for the 30- and 60-second film cuts (03, 05).
- **Who writes the color grade (LUT)** shared by the photographs and the film (04, 05).
- **Usage term** for photographs and film: perpetual, or the spec's five years renewable (04, 05).
- **The document-card props**: printed by the letterpress shop, or supplied by CoFile (04, 08).
- **The date of week 1**, which every brief counts from.
