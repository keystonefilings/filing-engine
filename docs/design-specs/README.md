# Ten visual designs for cofile.app (round two, Nov 26, 2026)

Ten fictional senior designers, each with a different age, background and craft, each wrote how
they would design the CoFile website with every graphic tool at their disposal. They worked
separately and never saw each other's work. Their brief is [`BRIEF.md`](BRIEF.md) (version 3):

- **The copy, the marketing tone and the selling brand are locked**: the round 3 copy now on the
  site, where the firm approves the proof, the home page is about 330 words and prices are public.
- **Every visual is theirs**: they could strip today's look entirely, and use more pictures or
  fewer, anywhere they liked. Only the words and their order are fixed.
- **Sell what the copy sells**: the firm's name, ease, nobody retyping, the papers coming back, a
  free rehearsal, simple prices. Security is one line and a link, not the story.

Round one (an earlier brief that led every designer to build the brand around safety and what
CoFile doesn't do) was discarded and is in git history only.

Each spec has the brief's 14 parts: concept, identity, color, type, layout, art direction, motion,
the home page section by section with every picture designed, the seven pages behind it and every
other surface (filing desk, the proof, client pages, dashboard, docs, emails), components, craft
notes, production, and what they'd strip from today's look. About 290,000 words in all. None uses
any other name for CoFile; the only prices are the site's own and real state fees.

## Where they agree

These came up independently in most or all of the ten, so treat them as close to settled.

1. **The firm section has its picture.** All ten show the same client page under two example firms
   (Harbor & Pine CPA and Northgate Payroll, from the site's own examples), with "with CoFile"
   identical and unmoved on both. Eight make "the door" literal somewhere: gilded glass or brass
   name plates (Hal, Rosa, Marcus, Tomás, Elaine, Amara) or a door picture between sections (Jun,
   Priya).
2. **"Ours in small type" is set literally small**: a 13 to 18 px "with CoFile" beside a very
   large firm name, on the home page and on every firm page.
3. **CoFile steps back so the firm's color leads.** Every design keeps CoFile to neutrals plus at
   most one accent, so the only strong color on a firm's pages is the firm's. Even the boldest
   (Amara, Rosa, Greta) go near-white on firm pages.
4. **The hero picture is Harbor & Pine's own filing desk**, real product UI in the firm's brand,
   with the Two Sisters Bakery row reading "Proof ready to approve".
5. **The pictures are the real product, live**, not screenshots: in HTML, in the example firm's
   brand, labeled as examples, sitting on photography, color fields or paper.
6. **Your own name comes next.** Four let the visitor type their firm's name onto the door or
   desk (Marcus, Elaine, Tomás, and Hal's wipe handle); Dev and Priya close the page on an empty
   plate or frame waiting for it; Elaine carries the typed name into sign-up.
7. **Rehearsal as theater**, from the copy's word: an empty stage, a ghost light, house lights
   dimming, a theater programme (Rosa, Tomás, Amara, Greta, Marcus).
8. **"$45" set as the biggest number on the page**: a 240 px figure, a rate card, a price board,
   a box-office strip.
9. **Real photos of the team** beside "Built and run by filing professionals": all ten ask for
   them.
10. **A commissioned CoFile wordmark.** Several give the "i" a square or paper-sheet dot; Greta
    and Amara both add a "Cº" monogram.

## Where they split (the real choices)

| Choice | Options, and who argues for each |
|---|---|
| **How much color CoFile itself has** | Bold color worlds on CoFile's own pages (Amara's marigold and aubergine, Rosa's main-street color, Greta's Poster Yellow) · Warm neutrals with one crafted accent (Hal's copper, Jun's persimmon, Elaine's gold, Tomás's lamplight) · Nearly colorless (Dev, Priya, Marcus) |
| **What the pictures are** | People: documentary or cut-out photography of firm owners and clients (Elaine, Rosa, Priya between sections) · Light and place: a desk or room in moving daylight (Jun, Tomás) · Crafted objects: engraved plates, gilded glass, sign painting (Hal, Rosa, Elaine, Tomás) · The product alone, staged (Dev, Marcus, Greta's typeset specimens) · 3D and kinetic type (Amara) |
| **Headline type** | Serif: Scotch roman, GT Alpina, Publico, Fraunces (Hal, Jun, Elaine, Tomás) · Grotesk: Suisse, GT America, a marquee or show-card gothic (Greta, Dev, Marcus, Priya, Amara, Rosa) |
| **The button color** | Yellow or marigold (Greta, Marcus, Amara) · Gold or amber (Elaine, Tomás) · Persimmon or flamboyán red (Jun, Rosa) · Copper (Hal) · The firm's own color, even on the home page's example (Dev, Priya) |
| **How much motion** | Real-time light across the whole scroll (Tomás), 3D and kinetic type (Amara) · One or two crafted moments, otherwise still (Hal, Elaine, Greta, Rosa, Marcus) · Calm, tactile motion throughout (Jun, Dev, Priya) |
| **Commissioned type** | A custom family (Hal, Amara, Rosa) · Licensed faces as they are, with only the wordmark commissioned (the rest) |

## What you'd need to provide or commission

- **Photos of real CoFile team members** for "Built and run by filing professionals": all ten
  designs. These must be real people who agree to appear.
- **The CoFile wordmark** (all ten), and for several, lettering for the example firms' doors.
- **Photography**: firm owners, clients and offices (Elaine, Rosa, Priya, Jun); doors and name
  plates (Hal, Rosa, Marcus, Tomás, Elaine); each spec has a shot list.
- **Craft work**: a sign painter or gilder, filmed (Rosa, Tomás, Elaine), or an engraver (Hal).
- **3D, WebGL or motion studios** for Amara and Tomás.
- **Interaction work** where the visitor types their own firm's name (four specs).

## Decisions the specs leave for you

- **Which direction, or a merge.** A merge round, like the copy reviews, would take the agreed
  points above as given and settle the splits.
- **New words.** The copy is locked, so anything the designs need that isn't written yet (alt text,
  a caption on the typed-name moment, labels on a pause button) is marked `[copy needed]` in each
  spec.
- **The team photos**: who appears, with their agreement.

## How to read them

- To see the firm's brand at work: the Firms part of section 9, and section 10 (the filing desk

- For photography and craft: section 7 of Elaine, Rosa and Hal.
- For motion: section 8 of Tomás, Amara and Jun.
