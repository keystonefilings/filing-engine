Please Note

You will need to upload a Certificate of Good Standing from your State of Incorporation to successfully file. If you do not have a Certificate of Good Standing at this time, you will need to obtain one before forming a business in the State of New Jersey.

The name you are filing must match the Certificate of Good Standing exactly, including designators. The Good Standing Certificate from the business entity's home state cannot be dated more than 30 days prior to today's date.

Failure to comply with this requirement will result in your filing being expunged and the forfeiture of your filing fees. If the exact name is not available, please call (609) 292-9292.
