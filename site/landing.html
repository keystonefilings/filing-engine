Workflow

From intake to filing records

  1. Accept the order

    Send entity details through the API. Apply the registered agent, signer and other defaults you set for that client.

  2. Collect approval

    Send a review link under your brand. The designated approver confirms the filing details and fees.

  3. Run on the portal

    Match fields against the live government form and check charges against the approved amount before payment.

  4. Return the record

    Receive status updates in your system, with references to filing documents when the agency makes them available.

Filing controls

Review exceptions before submission

  • Pause on form changes

    If a required field or option changes, the engine pauses the run for review.

  • Keep charges within approval

    If the portal fee falls outside the approved amount, the filing stops before payment.

  • Confirm the outcome before retrying

    If a run is interrupted at submission, it goes to reconciliation for someone to confirm the outcome.

Subscriber setup

Operate under your own brand and accounts

Company-specific workspace

Your filing jobs and documents are scoped to your company. The worker retrieves portal credentials from a company-specific vault when a run starts.

Client-specific rules

Set defaults for each firm you serve and lock answers that need to stay consistent across its filings.

Branded approvals

Review pages display your name and support contact. Links are single-use and expire. Social Security numbers are masked on the page.

Filing history

Review recorded portal steps and trace whether a value came from intake, client settings or a company default.

API and webhooks

Connect to the systems you already use

Create filing requests from your website, CRM or back office. Signed webhooks report approval, exceptions and filing results to your system, with delivery retries when your endpoint is unavailable.
